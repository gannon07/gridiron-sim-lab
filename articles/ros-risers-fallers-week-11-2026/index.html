Risers, Fallers and Trade Targets After Week 10

Davante Adams and Carnell Tate climbed the most in our rest-of-season rankings since October 30, while Michael Wilson is out for the season and Puka Nacua likely is too. Plus three players to trade for and three to sell.

Every day the model plays out the rest of the NFL season 10,000 times, and our rest-of-season rankings come from those runs. This is our first Risers and Fallers post since October 30, so it compares Friday's rankings with the ones from that day. That covers three weeks of games, from the rest of Week 8 through Thursday night's Colts at Texans game. Rankings are half PPR for a 12-team league.

We left quarterbacks off these lists. A quarterback's ranking mostly moves with who's starting, so the list would just be a depth chart. We also only list players who were in the top 36 receivers, top 30 running backs or top 15 tight ends in one of the two weeks, because deep ranks are bunched so close together that small changes swing them.

How We Count Byes and Injuries

When one of your players is on bye or hurt, you don't take a zero. You start someone off waivers. So our rankings give every player what a waiver pickup would score in his bye week and in the games we expect him to miss. A missed game only costs him the gap between himself and the fill-in.

Davante Adams, Rams. He's still out with his hamstring injury, but his coach says he could come back after the Week 12 game against Green Bay. With Puka Nacua likely out for the season (see the fallers), the model expects Adams to get more of the targets once he's back. It now expects about 15.4 points a game from him when he plays, up from 10.7.

Carnell Tate, Titans. He's missed two games with a shoulder injury. He was back at practice on a limited basis this week, and his coach says he has a good chance to play Sunday at Dallas. He practiced fully Friday and is listed as questionable. The model expects about 8 targets a game from him and 12.7 points a game from here, up from 10.7.

Nico Collins, Texans. He's caught a touchdown in each of his last three games, including 9 catches for 94 yards on 16 targets against Cleveland in Week 10.

DeVonta Smith, Eagles. His own projection barely moved (about 11.5 points a game). He climbed because receivers who were ahead of him on October 30 fell, starting with Nacua and Michael Wilson.

Tony Pollard, Titans. 21 carries, 117 yards and two touchdowns against Cincinnati, then 8 catches for 66 yards and a touchdown against Jacksonville. The model has him at about 14.4 points a game from here, up from 12.8.

Chuba Hubbard and Bucky Irving. Hubbard ran 27 times for 92 yards and two touchdowns against New Orleans and added 5 catches for 80 yards. The model now has him at about 16.2 points a game. Irving had 170 rushing yards against Atlanta and two touchdowns against Chicago before Tampa Bay's bye.

Luther Burden III and Garrett Wilson. Burden scored in Weeks 8 and 9 on 19 targets. Wilson was on our fallers list two straight times in October, and he's back up to WR19.

Harold Fannin Jr., Browns. 10 catches for 154 yards on 12 targets against Pittsburgh, then 7 for 91 against Houston.

Michael Wilson, Cardinals. He'll miss the rest of the season with a neck injury.

Puka Nacua, Rams. He had knee surgery, and his coach says he'll miss multiple months, likely the rest of the season. The league also suspended him three games. Our rankings now treat him as done for this season. If you need the roster spot, you can let him go.

Zay Flowers, Ravens. No catches against Buffalo, then 3 catches for 6 yards against Jacksonville. He bounced back with 6 catches for 74 yards against the Chargers, but the model now has him at about 10.3 points a game, down from 13.6.

Xavier Worthy, Chiefs. He hurt his hamstring against the Jets in Week 9, then had no catches on 3 targets against Atlanta. He has no injury designation now. The model has him at about 7.7 points a game.

Travis Etienne, Saints. He came back from his hamstring injury in Week 10 and ran 8 times for 14 yards against Carolina. The model expects about 6 carries a game from him now.

Kyren Williams, Rams. He's run for 124 yards over his last three games, with only 23 on 12 carries against Arizona. The model has him at about 13.8 points a game, down from 15.9.

Jeremiyah Love, Cardinals. He's on injured reserve with a high ankle sprain and is expected to be out two to three more weeks.

Ashton Jeanty, Raiders. 17 carries in his last three games combined. He still catches passes, with 15 targets in that stretch, so the model has him at about 15.3 points a game. That's still a solid starter.

Buy Low

These compare two ranks for each player: where he ranks in points per game so far this season, and where the model ranks him for the rest of the season. A buy low is a player the model ranks well above his points so far. Points per game come from the nflverse weekly stats, counting players with at least three games.

KC Concepcion, Browns. He was a buy low on October 30 and still is. He's WR45 in points per game (8.0 over 10 games) but WR21 for the rest of the season. The model expects about 7 targets and 11.9 points a game from here.

Saquon Barkley, Eagles. He's RB41 in points per game (7.9 over seven games) and RB21 for the rest of the season. The model expects about 18 carries a game from him and 11.6 points a game from here.

Tyler Warren, Colts. He's TE13 in points per game (7.7 over nine games) and TE3 for the rest of the season. The model expects about 8 targets and 12.0 points a game from him. If his manager sees him as a mid-range tight end, ask.

Sell High

Deebo Samuel Sr., 49ers. He's WR16 in points per game (12.8 over six games) but WR43 for the rest of the season. The model expects about 6 targets a game from him and 8.0 points a game from here.

Blake Corum, Rams. He's RB24 in points per game (10.9 over 10 games) and RB35 for the rest of the season. He's been on the field for about a third of the Rams' snaps, and the model has him at about 8.8 points a game from here.

Mike Gesicki, Bengals. He's TE7 in points per game (9.0 over eight games) but TE28 for the rest of the season. The model expects about 4 targets a game and 6.0 points a game.
