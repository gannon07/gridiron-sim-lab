November 27, 2026

Risers, Fallers and Trade Targets After Week 11

Jonah Coleman jumped 14 spots in our rest-of-season rankings and DeVonta Smith rose after a 200-yard Thanksgiving, while J.K. Dobbins and Nico Collins slid. Plus three players to trade for and three to sell.

Every day the model plays out the rest of the NFL season 10,000 times, and our rest-of-season rankings come from those runs. This post compares Friday's rankings with the ones from last Friday, November 20. That covers the rest of Week 11 plus Week 12's Wednesday and Thanksgiving games. Friday's Broncos at Steelers game isn't in yet. Rankings are half PPR for a 12-team league.

We left quarterbacks off these lists. A quarterback's ranking mostly moves with who's starting, so the list would just be a depth chart. We also only list players who were in the top 36 receivers, top 30 running backs or top 15 tight ends in one of the two weeks, because deep ranks are bunched so close together that small changes swing them.

How We Count Byes and Injuries

When one of your players is on bye or hurt, you don't take a zero. You start someone off waivers. So our rankings give every player what a waiver pickup would score in his bye week and in the games we expect him to miss. A missed game only costs him the gap between himself and the fill-in.

Jonah Coleman, Broncos. He ran 24 times for 111 yards and three touchdowns against the Raiders in Week 11. Then Denver suspended J.K. Dobbins for Friday's game at Pittsburgh. The model now expects about 15 carries a game from Coleman and 10.3 points a game from here, up from 7.4.

DeVonta Smith, Eagles. He came back from his hamstring injury in Week 11, and on Thanksgiving he caught 11 passes for 200 yards and two touchdowns at Dallas on 14 targets. The model expects about 10 targets a game from him and 12.6 points a game from here, up from 11.4.

Parker Washington, Jaguars. 17 targets against the Giants in Week 11, with 8 catches for 103 yards. He was limited in practice Thursday with a shoulder injury. The model has him at about 8 targets and 12.1 points a game, up from 11.2.

DJ Moore, Bills. 8 catches for 91 yards and a touchdown on 10 targets against Miami in Week 11. Thanksgiving was quiet (2 catches for 15 yards on 6 targets against Kansas City), but the model still nudged him up to about 10.1 points a game from 9.7.

KC Concepcion and Matthew Golden. Their own projections barely moved (about 12.2 and 12.6 points a game). They climbed because receivers who were ahead of them last week slipped. You'll find a few of those in the fallers.

Jeremiyah Love, Cardinals. He's still on injured reserve with a high ankle sprain and is out Sunday against Washington. When he plays, the model has him at about 14.6 points a game. He moved up because he's a week closer to coming back, so there are fewer games left for him to miss.

Harold Fannin Jr., Browns. He moved up to full practice Thursday with a groin injury. Tight ends who were ahead of him last week, George Kittle and Isaiah Likely, slipped a couple of spots.

J.K. Dobbins, Broncos. His coach says the team suspended him for Friday's game at Pittsburgh. He had 1 carry against the Raiders while Coleman ran for three touchdowns. The model has him at about 8.7 points a game from here, down from 10.3.

Nico Collins, Texans. A week after he was one of our biggest risers, he got 5 targets against the Colts, down from 16 against Cleveland the week before. He still caught a touchdown. The model has him at about 11.5 points a game, down from 12.6.

Rachaad White, Commanders. He was listed as questionable with a heel injury for Monday's game against Cincinnati and didn't get a carry or a catch. The model has him at about 8.3 points a game, down from 9.9.

Tee Higgins, Bengals. 3 targets against Washington in Week 11, after 6 in each of the two games before. He made them count with 43 yards and a touchdown, but the model has him at about 11.6 points a game, down from 12.3. He's also on our sell list below.

Luther Burden III, Bears. Since he came back from his concussion, he has 5 catches for 54 yards on 10 targets over two games. The model has him at about 11.3 points a game, down from 12.

DK Metcalf, Steelers. 1 catch for 2 yards on 4 targets against the Eagles in Week 11. The model has him at about 9 points a game, down from 10.4.

Kenneth Walker III, Chiefs. He missed Thanksgiving at Buffalo with a groin injury, his third straight game out. When he plays, the model still has him at about 17 points a game. He slid because the missed games keep adding up.

Tetairoa McMillan, Panthers. He didn't practice Thursday with a foot injury. His projection when he plays hasn't changed (about 11.4 points a game). The drop is about the chance he misses time.

Buy Low

These compare two ranks for each player: where he ranks in points per game so far this season, and where the model ranks him for the rest of the season. A buy low is a player the model ranks well above his points so far. Points per game come from the nflverse weekly stats, counting players with at least three games.

Carnell Tate, Titans. He's WR32 in points per game (9.1 over eight games) but WR11 for the rest of the season. He has 2 touchdowns on 36 catches, and the model expects about 8.5 targets and 12.7 points a game from here.

Chase Brown, Bengals. He's RB24 in points per game (11.2 over 10 games) and RB8 for the rest of the season. He has 52 catches already but only 3 touchdowns. The model expects about 12.5 carries and 6 targets a game, and 15.0 points a game from here.

Tyler Warren, Colts. He was a buy low last week and still is. He's TE12 in points per game (7.7 over nine games) and TE4 for the rest of the season. The model expects about 7 targets and 11.3 points a game from him.

Sell High

D'Andre Swift, Bears. He's RB9 in points per game (16.2 over 11 games) but RB17 for the rest of the season. He has 12 touchdowns in 11 games, and that pace is hard to keep up. The model expects about 15 carries and 13.3 points a game from here.

Tee Higgins, Bengals. He's WR8 in points per game (15.6 over 10 games) and WR17 for the rest of the season. Seven of his 55 catches went for touchdowns. The model expects about 8 targets and 11.6 points a game.

Pat Freiermuth, Steelers. He's TE10 in points per game (8.3 over 10 games) but TE21 for the rest of the season. Six touchdowns have carried his season, and the model expects about 4 targets and 6.7 points a game from here.
